I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at The University of Manchester and the National Centre for Text Mining (NaCTeM), supervised by Prof. Sophia Ananiadou. I am partly sponsored by BAE Systems.

I study large language models and agentic systems for software and systems engineering, with a broader interest in reliable AI in high-risk domains. My work also explores financial NLP and multilingual evaluation.

Janus: A Benchmark for Goal-Conditioned Information Distortion in LLMs

Polydoros Giannouris, Mohsinul Kabir, Sophia Ananiadou

LLMs can mislead without technically lying. We introduce Janus, a benchmark for measuring how models reshape the same underlying evidence to create misleading impressions when pursuing an institutional goal.

NOMAD: A Multi-Agent LLM System for UML Class Diagram Generation from Natural Language Requirements

Polydoros Giannouris, Sophia Ananiadou

Turning written requirements into a UML diagram involves several distinct decisions. We introduce NOMAD, a multi-agent framework that distributes these decisions across specialized LLM agents to improve modeling performance and systematically characterize remaining errors.

Semantic Label Drift in Cross-Cultural Translation

Mohsinul Kabir, Tasnim Ahmed, Md Mezbaur Rahman, Polydoros Giannouris, Sophia Ananiadou

A translated text can acquire a different sentiment label. We study how cultural differences drive this shift and why an LLM’s cultural knowledge can sometimes amplify it.

Improving Multilabel Text Emotion Detection with Emotion Interrelation Anchors

Polydoros Giannouris, Vasileios Mygdalis, Ioannis Pitas

Emotions often occur together, yet classifiers typically predict each one independently. We introduce emotion anchors that help each classifier account for these relationships, including few-shot domain adaptation.
